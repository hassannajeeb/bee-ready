# Bee Ready

A practice app for the **Pakistan Spelling Bee 2026–27** (English), Grades 3 and 5.

**Open the app:** https://hassannajeeb.github.io/bee-ready/

Bee Ready reads each word aloud, checks the spelling, and runs mock rounds that follow the PSB Regional Championship rules. It runs in the browser, with nothing to install.

## What it does

- **Practice.** Hear a word, then type it or spell it out loud. Missed words are marked "tricky" and come back first next time. A word counts as mastered after two correct spellings in a row.
- **Mock bee.** Three rounds of 10 words: 7 from the official list and 3 from a stand-in hidden list. Each word has a 40-second timer with a signal at 20 seconds. Scoring follows the PSB rules (+2, 0, −1), and you need 16 points after Round 2 to reach Round 3.
- **Listening.** The app can listen while the speller says the word, spells it, and says it again (Say–SPELL–Say). It checks the spelling and the procedure, and flags sounded-out syllables. A grown-up can change any score.
- **Word bank.** Every word on the list, colour-coded by progress. Tap a word to hear it.
- **Several spellers.** Each child keeps their own progress.

## Using it

| | Chrome / Edge | Safari (Mac, iPad) | Firefox |
|---|---|---|---|
| Practice and mock bee | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Listening mode | ✓ | ✓ (turn on Dictation in system settings) | ✗ |

- **Pronunciations** are played from Merriam-Webster's website, so the app needs an internet connection. Words without a recording use the device's built-in voice.
- **Listening** uses the browser's speech recognition. Chrome and Edge send the audio to Google or Microsoft to be recognised. The app itself never records or stores audio.
- **Progress** is saved only in the browser on that device. Clearing the browser's site data erases it, and progress doesn't carry over between devices or browsers.
- **Settings** on the home screen let you switch between Merriam-Webster recordings and the device voice, and change the accent and speaking speed.

## Good to know

- **The hidden list is a stand-in.** PSB does not publish its hidden list, so the mock bee uses other grade-level words that are not on the official list.
- **British and American spellings** are both accepted, as in the competition (for example *colourful* / *colorful*).
- **SpellTok tiebreakers** are not included.
